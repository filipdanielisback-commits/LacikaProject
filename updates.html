NOVEMBER 26, 2026 · DEVELOPMENT UPDATE

A new priority: Moto G60 boot-port research

LacikaProject is shifting its immediate development focus toward researching an independent Darwin/XNU boot port for the Motorola Moto G60.

Our first target remains the Moto G60, codename hanoip. We are planning our own boot architecture, loader components and device-specific platform support.

What we're focusing on

  • LacikaBoot: Researching the boot architecture.
  • LacikaXNULoader: Planning our independent loader.
  • LacikaXNU: Investigating Apple's public XNU source and platform requirements.
  • LacikaDrivers: Researching hardware support for hanoip.

What about QEMU?

QEMU development is temporarily paused while we prioritize phone-port research. It remains part of our future development plans and has not been cancelled.

Current status

Research phase — not yet booting on the Moto G60. No working phone boot, completed port or bootable release is being announced.

NOVEMBER 25, 2026

LacikaProject development started

The initial project repository, documentation structure and website development were started. Early work also began on Darwin/XNU research and the LacikaWiFi driver research scaffold.
