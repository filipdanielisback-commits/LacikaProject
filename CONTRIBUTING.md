# Contributing to LacikaProject

Thank you for your interest in contributing.

## Before contributing

- Read the project [README](README.md) and relevant documentation in `docs/`.
- Check existing issues and pull requests before starting substantial work.
- For large changes, open an issue first to discuss the proposed direction.

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a focused branch for your change.
3. Make a small, clearly scoped update.
4. Check spelling, links, formatting, and technical accuracy.
5. Submit a pull request explaining what changed and how you checked it.

## Research and technical claims

- Distinguish verified results from hypotheses and plans.
- Include device model, software version, test conditions, and relevant logs when safe to share.
- Do not describe a device as supported unless the relevant functionality has actually been tested.
- Do not claim that Darwin/XNU research provides a complete iOS distribution or Apple's proprietary services.

## Safety and privacy

Do not commit passwords, access tokens, private keys, personal data, or other secrets. Do not upload another person's files or logs containing identifying information without permission.

## Style

Prefer clear documentation, descriptive filenames, and focused commits. Explain technical terms when they may be unfamiliar to readers.
