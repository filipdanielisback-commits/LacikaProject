PROJECT HISTORY

Changelog

Development milestones, project changes and research progress.

NOVEMBER 26, 2026

New development priority: Moto G60 boot-port research

LacikaProject is shifting its immediate development focus toward researching an independent Darwin/XNU boot port for the Motorola Moto G60.

Development priorities

  • Begin planning the LacikaBoot architecture.
  • Research the LacikaXNULoader design.
  • Investigate XNU platform requirements for hanoip.
  • Research device-specific hardware and driver support.

Project status changes

  • First research target remains Motorola Moto G60 (hanoip).
  • QEMU development is temporarily paused, not cancelled.
  • Phone boot-port work is in the research phase.
  • No working phone boot or bootable release is claimed.

This is a change in development priorities, not an announcement of a completed port.

NOVEMBER 25, 2026

Project foundation

  • Created the LacikaProject repository foundation.
  • Organized initial project documentation.
  • Started the experimental Darwin/XNU research.
  • Started LacikaWiFi driver research.
  • Continued development of the project website.

IMPORTANT

Development transparency

LacikaProject is an independent experimental research project. Research milestones do not mean that a device port is functional. Hardware compatibility, kernel boot and driver functionality must be tested and documented before being marked as verified.
