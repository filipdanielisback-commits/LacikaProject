**Status: research notes; not a complete or independently verified hardware inventory.**

## Current research record

- Device family: Motorola Moto G60.
- Codename used by the project: `hanoip`.
- SoC family recorded by the project: Qualcomm Snapdragon 732G.
- Target memory configuration: 6 GB.
- Exact model and board revision must accompany any hardware-specific claim.

## Evidence rules

For each component, record the source document, exact model/board revision, publication date and confidence. Prefer manufacturer documentation and reliable device-specific technical references.

Do not guess:

- wireless chipset or bus interface;
- register addresses or memory maps;
- firmware filenames or versions;
- device-tree values;
- power-management or display-controller details.
