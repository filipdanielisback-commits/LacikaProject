# Device Research Status

A device appearing in this document means it is being considered or researched. It does not mean that a working port exists.

## First research target

| Manufacturer | Device | Codename | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Motorola | Moto G60 | hanoip | Experimental research; support not verified |

## Future targets

Additional devices may be considered later. Their exact variants, codenames, and hardware details must be checked before being documented as confirmed.

## Status definitions

- **Experimental research:** investigation is in progress; functionality is not established.
- **Planned:** a possible future research target.
- **Not verified:** no reproducible test evidence has been recorded.
- **Verified:** only use this label for a specific feature that has been tested and documented.

Do not use “supported” as a general label without clear test evidence and a description of what works.
