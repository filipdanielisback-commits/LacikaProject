# Project Overview

## What is LacikaProject?

LacikaProject is an independent experimental research project exploring Darwin/XNU-related development in the context of Android devices.

## Goals

- Learn about relevant operating-system architecture.
- Document hardware and boot-chain research.
- Record compatibility questions and experimental results.
- Share accurate documentation and project progress.

## Non-goals and limitations

The project does not claim to provide a complete iOS installation, Apple's proprietary software or services, or a working port for any Android device. Such compatibility must not be assumed.

## First research target

The initial research target is the Motorola Moto G60 (codename `hanoip`). Its inclusion is a research designation, not a statement of verified support.

## Reporting progress

Progress notes should label each statement as a plan, hypothesis, experiment, or verified result. Include evidence where practical.
