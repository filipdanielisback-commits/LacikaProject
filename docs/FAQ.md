# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is LacikaProject a complete iOS port?

No. The project explores Darwin/XNU-related research. A kernel alone is not a complete iOS distribution, and Apple's proprietary components and services are not provided.

## Does it currently work on the Motorola Moto G60?

The Moto G60 (hanoip) is the first research target. A working port and hardware support have not been verified.

## Does listing a device mean it is supported?

No. Device listings identify research targets or plans only.

## Where can I find development results?

Results will be documented in this repository as experiments are performed and reviewed.

## Can I contribute?

Yes. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md) before opening a pull request.

## Is the project affiliated with Apple or Motorola?

No. LacikaProject is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple, Motorola, or other manufacturers.
