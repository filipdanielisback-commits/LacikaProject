IMPORTANT

Project status and licensing

LacikaProject is experimental Darwin/XNU research. It is not a complete iOS distribution, and no working Moto G60 port or bootable image is claimed here.

A repository being publicly viewable does not automatically grant permission to reuse its code. Check the repository's current licence and the licence of every dependency before copying, modifying or redistributing code. A project licence has not yet been selected.
