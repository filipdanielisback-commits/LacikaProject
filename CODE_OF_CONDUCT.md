# Community Code of Conduct

LacikaProject aims to be a respectful, constructive space for learning and technical collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical work, not personal characteristics.
- Welcome beginners and explain disagreements clearly.
- Give useful, specific feedback.
- Respect privacy and only share material you have permission to share.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately misleading contributors about project capabilities or test results.
- Spam or disruptive behavior.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior in this project, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub with relevant context.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or decline contributions that do not follow these guidelines.
